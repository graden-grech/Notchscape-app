# Civic artwork prompts

Built-in image_gen, 2 November 2026. The existing White House Exterior image was the style reference. Each separate 16:9 generated architectural scene requested the same deep navy blue-hour sky, delicate clouds, warm amber architectural uplighting, realistic stone and dark landscaped foreground, whole recognizable building within frame with shallow-menu crop safety, strongest architecture center/right and calm dark space to the left. No text, watermark, UI, party emblems or added people. These are generated artwork, not documentary photographs or verified architectural surveys.

1. Treasury: United States Treasury Building in Washington, D.C., three-quarter pedestrian viewpoint, long classical colonnade and Alexander Hamilton statue.
2. Pentagon: low elevated aerial three-quarter viewpoint showing its five-sided plan and central courtyard.
3. Eisenhower Executive Office Building: three-quarter street viewpoint, elaborate Second Empire architecture and steep mansard roofs.

The unmodified generated masters are retained in source-assets. No competitor artwork or code was imported. This folder is source artwork for a future separately packaged collection; it is not a published or installed download.
