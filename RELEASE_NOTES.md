# boringNotch Custom 2.8.0

- Shows a single Apple-style green completion check on one side of the notch.
- Detects AI usage and rate limits, shows a red three-second alert, and clears
  abandoned working states automatically.
- Adds an optional System tab with efficient, on-demand CPU, memory, storage,
  and thermal-pressure monitoring plus safe Mac controls.
- Adds rounded rectangle, squircle, and capsule mirror shapes.
- Introduces a new Liquid Glass app icon, native Apple Liquid Glass navigation
  and music controls, plus translucent Settings, Live, System, and Shelf surfaces.
- Replaces the separate top buttons with a compact Apple-style segmented
  control and removes the stray pressed-symbol animation from music controls.
- Keeps the HUD enabled across launches and rebuilds, automatically restarts
  its media-key interceptor after Accessibility approval, and uses a stable
  local signing identity so macOS keeps the permission grant.
- Removes costly activation-policy switching when Settings opens and closes,
  making the custom Settings window significantly smoother.
- Keeps Sparkle automatic updates on the public
  `graden-grech/boringNotch-custom` GitHub feed.

- Adds Codex and Claude Code activities with provider colors, Music/AI priority,
  dual-activity layouts, and four-second completion indicators.
- Adds a location-aware weather tile that can live on Home or the Live page.
- Restores native notch sizing when weather is on the Live page and keeps the
  calendar at its normal size.
- Adds Live Activity controls, timers, download status, calendar alerts, and
  Apple-style privacy indicators.
- Improves the HUD permission flow and keeps the original rounded notch shape.
- Refines the AI completion checkmark and music waveform with a cleaner,
  Apple-like appearance.
- Moves Sparkle updates to the public `graden-grech/boringNotch-custom` feed.
