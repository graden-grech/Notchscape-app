Welcome to Notchscape

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later. Native Liquid Glass is available on macOS 26 or later.

This installer puts Notchscape in the shared Applications folder, available to every account on this Mac.

Each person keeps their own settings and decides whether to open the app at login. macOS permissions are requested separately in that person’s account; the installer does not grant them.

For a new user, Weather starts off and Show on all displays starts on. Updating preserves existing preferences.

Please quit Notchscape before continuing. macOS may ask an administrator to approve installing in Applications.
