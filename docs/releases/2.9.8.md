## Desktop Snow & Seasons

- Six desktop weather presets, including distinct Winter Dawn and Holiday Night effects.
- Adjust the bottom buildup and base layer, place snow or leaf piles, and arrange seasonal scenery.
- Choose a Mac desktop wallpaper for each season. Automatic matching is optional.

## Dock and appearance

- Dock Bar uses the display's bottom edge after its macOS Dock suppression lease is applied.
- Open Minimized Windows from a running app's Dock Bar menu to restore a specific window using existing Accessibility access.
- Drag the native Dock icon-size and Home tile-transparency sliders continuously.

Face Unlock is excluded from this public download. Requires Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later. This archive is development-signed and is not notarized.
