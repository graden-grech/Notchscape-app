# Notchscape 2.9.4 (297) — Cityscapes and a better notch fit

- Cityscapes adds twelve separate city scenes using the original White House
  exterior's navy blue-hour sky and warm architectural light: New York, Chicago,
  Detroit, Boston, Philadelphia, Washington D.C., Los Angeles, San Francisco,
  Seattle, Las Vegas, Miami and New Orleans. Choose them from one Cityscapes
  button in Appearance, with complete-photo previews and a remembered city.
- City themes use the same Home background and header as White House. Manual
  accent and app-icon choices remain independent; saved appearances can include
  a city. All 24 White House room, color and Christmas combinations keep the
  same corrected physical-notch alignment.
- The hardware cover uses the measurements of its own display and stays at the
  screen's top edge. The controls retain their top spacing. Extra software notch
  width and the artificial black top-edge line are removed.
- Battery warnings stay in visible lanes beside the camera housing. Normal
  charging and power changes use one symbol and a percentage; low and critical
  alerts keep concise text. Alert thresholds and notchless settings are preserved.
- Today's date stays readable on bright theme and custom accent colors.

The active project, download and signed update feed are in
[Notchscape-app](https://github.com/graden-grech/Notchscape-app). Older repository
feeds retain their signed 2.9.3 migration item; after that update, the app checks
this repository for later versions. Historical downloads are retained.

Requires Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later; native Liquid Glass requires
macOS 26. Accounts remain disabled. App/helper signing identity and the existing
update key are preserved. The app is Apple Development signed, not Developer ID
signed or notarized; no unsigned installer preview is distributed.

The release's verification assets distinguish signed-build, live UI, real
updater/HUD and hosted CI checks. Other hardware/macOS versions, DisplayLink,
fresh-account permissions and complete accessibility/performance coverage remain
incomplete. Original-source and dependency notices remain available.

Artwork provenance and hashes are in
[the Cityscapes record](https://github.com/graden-grech/Notchscape-app/blob/v2.9.4/docs/CITYSCAPES_ARTWORK.json).
