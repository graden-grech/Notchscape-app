# Notchscape 2.9.3 (296) — New repository and update destination

The active project and new releases move to
[graden-grech/Notchscape-app](https://github.com/graden-grech/Notchscape-app), a
new standalone repository with fresh history. The app remains **Notchscape**.

- New builds check the new repository's signed Sparkle feed.
- Existing Notchscape feeds provide the signed migration update, while older
  release/download URLs remain available.
- App/helper signing identity, update key, saved settings and permissions remain
  the same. Accounts remain disabled.
- Includes the optional six-chapter setup with Skip/Back/Next and replay from
  General or Help; 24 White House scene combinations; red/blue and Christmas
  icons; improved header spacing; and the earlier features.
- New users start with Weather off and Show on all displays on. Existing users'
  explicit and old implicit settings are retained.

Requires Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later; native Liquid Glass requires
macOS 26. The app is Apple Development signed, not Developer ID signed or
notarized. The unsigned installer preview is not distributed. Full other-OS,
hardware/DisplayLink, fresh-account permissions and accessibility/performance
coverage remain incomplete.

Published archive hashes, source revision, independent signature verification,
hosted CI and real updater/HUD outcomes are recorded separately in the release's
verification assets. A source push alone does not establish those results.
See [migration details](../NEW_REPOSITORY_MIGRATION.md) and
[distribution readiness](../DISTRIBUTION_READINESS.md). Original-source licenses
and notices are preserved.
