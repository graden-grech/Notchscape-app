# Notchscape 2.9.2 (293) — Local White House preview

Installed locally on 26 October 2026 at `/Applications/Notchscape.app`.
This preview is not published. The public 2.9.1 release and historical update
feed entries remain unchanged.

A subsequent signed local build **294** adds Republican/Democrat White House
icons and a Christmas version for each. Its installed verification and artwork
records are in [the icon follow-up](../WHITE_HOUSE_ICON_ARTWORK.md). The hashes
and package checks below remain the distinct build-293 artifacts; they must not
be presented as including the newer icons.

## Changes

- White House has 24 bundled scenes: four rooms/views, three photo/accent
  styles, and an independent Christmas option. Red and blue variants change
  the actual décor/lighting. Festive exteriors add snow and lights.
- Room and theme pickers show complete photos at their natural aspect ratio.
  Theme cards share a row height. Home retains its shallow panorama and
  calendar layout, with a vertically centered architectural crop.
- Expanded Home controls have eight points of top clearance, including Mirror,
  Settings and Battery. Closed presentation and menu height are unchanged.
- New users start with Weather off and Show on all displays on. Existing
  explicit settings and completed users' former implicit defaults are preserved.
- A native installer can target shared `/Applications`, making the app bundle
  available to every local account. Settings, privacy grants and login startup
  remain per account. See [shared installation](../SHARED_INSTALLATION.md).

## Remaining release checks

This is Apple Development signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized. The
unsigned package is an unpublished structural preview, not a customer installer.
No privileged package installation or second-account launch was performed.
Actual second-account permissions remain that account's responsibility.

key's signature and independent verification, user-authorized publication, and a
real update/relaunch test. Hosted CI has not run on these unpushed commits.

modes and animation/performance profiling remain separate. Still-image checks
