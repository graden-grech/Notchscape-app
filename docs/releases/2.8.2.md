# boringNotch Custom 2.8.2

- Adds optional Enhanced battery controls with a cleaner popover, real local battery history, icon/percentage display choices, and brief optional 20%, 10%, and full-charge alerts. Original battery style and its preferences remain available.
- Corrects battery percentage normalization and charging-status labels, avoids misleading battery-health values, and hides the battery indicator on Macs without a built-in battery unless overridden.
- Adds a Show Dock icon setting and prevents hidden Dock artwork updates. macOS may retain pinned or recent Dock shortcuts; remove those separately.
- Adds customizable Home widget ordering and visibility, Quick Actions for apps and Apple Shortcuts, and optional local AI outcome history.
- Adds Balanced, Low impact, and automatic battery-conscious rendering modes.
- Refines seasonal Home backgrounds, adds an inclusive Winter theme, separates manual icon selection from theme matching, and consolidates accent and icon settings.
- Adds opt-in seasonal theme switching, optional holiday themes, and manual accent-color customization.
- Improves calendar contrast, header layout, and interrupted AI outcome handling.
- Keeps the Enhanced battery glass inside the Home header, including when charging.
- Uses Apple's native small-size switches throughout Settings and onboarding, including the system-provided Liquid Glass appearance and interaction on macOS 26. No custom switch tracks or thumb animation.
- Uses lighter standard materials for content cards, respects Reduce Transparency, and stops decorative onboarding effects with Reduce Motion or Low impact mode.
- Improves settings window resizing and toolbar separation while scrolling, and adds accessible labels/actions for media controls, Mirror, file sharing, and opening the notch menu.
- Keeps app-build and security checks enabled while making unconfigured upstream website/nightly services opt-in.

## Requirements and validation

Apple silicon Mac, macOS 14 or later. Native Liquid Glass requires macOS 26 or later.

Development-signed with the existing identity; not notarized. The updater retains the existing custom Sparkle public key; published update archives are verified against it. Local builds, 87 customization-policy checks, 4 real power-source checks, and 43 workflow/helper tests passed. All 15 settings sections were visually reviewed, including long-page scrolling and native switch accessibility/interaction. Home, Live, Shelf, and the charging battery indicator/popover were checked on the connected display. Original/Enhanced switching, real battery history, and hidden Dock startup were checked locally. A saved recent Dock entry was removed separately with permission; the app does not rewrite Dock settings automatically.

Real 20%/10%/full-charge transitions, a complete HUD permission/restart regression, VoiceOver end-to-end testing, onboarding permission dialogs, and comprehensive external-display/DisplayLink performance testing remain pending. Battery history begins with actual observations while Enhanced is active; the app does not fabricate older readings. This review is not an Apple certification or a guarantee that every configuration matches iOS.
